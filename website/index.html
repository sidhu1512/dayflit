Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Dayflit's local architecture, security, shortcuts, and features.

Does Dayflit require an account or an active internet connection? +

No. Dayflit operates 100% offline by default. There are zero mandatory sign-ups, no authentication cookies, and zero diagnostic telemetry. The internet is only used for optional integrations you explicitly enable, such as Google Calendar or Spotify.

Where are my files and tasks stored on my PC? +

All tasks, notes, journal entries, and settings are stored as plain, human-readable JSON files in a directory of your choice (defaulting to your user AppData or a custom sync directory like OneDrive, Dropbox, or Syncthing). Automatic daily zip archives protect against file loss.

How does offline Whisper voice dictation work? +

When you hold AltShiftV, Dayflit captures audio through your selected microphone and runs inference using an optimized, lightweight local Whisper model directly on your CPU or GPU. Your voice recordings never touch an external server.

Can I customize the global shortcuts? +

Yes. Dayflit defaults to conflict-free shortcuts (AltSpace for the companion, AltShiftT for task capture, AltShiftV for voice memo, AltShiftSpace for focus), but you can rebind every global hotkey inside Settings → Shortcuts.

Can I export my journal and notes to Obsidian? +

Yes! Dayflit includes a one-click Markdown Vault export in Settings → Data. It writes clean Markdown files with YAML front matter for dates, tags, and moods, organized cleanly in folders that Obsidian, Logseq, or VS Code can open immediately.

Does the distraction reminder forcefully block apps? +

No. Dayflit respects your agency. When a flagged distraction app (like Steam or a social media tab) comes into focus during an active Pomodoro block, Dayflit shows a quiet acrylic banner displaying your remaining focus countdown. Nothing is blocked; you remain in control.
