Most productivity apps want your whole screen. Dayflit is designed to sit beside your work, not on top of it.

Dayflit stores everything as plain JSON files with checksums in a folder you choose. Put it in OneDrive or Dropbox to carry it between PCs. Daily zip backups, corruption recovery, and a one-click Markdown export that opens as an Obsidian vault.

  • Signed automatic updates, installed in place

Questions

Good to know

Does Dayflit need an account or an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline. The internet is only used for things you opt into: calendar feeds, Google/Outlook sync, Spotify, link previews and update checks.

Where is my data?

In a folder you choose, as plain JSON with daily zip backups. Move it to OneDrive or Dropbox from Settings → Data to use Dayflit on several PCs.

How does voice transcription work?

Hold the voice key and speak. Dayflit records locally and transcribes with an offline Whisper model you download once (75–470 MB), or with Windows speech recognition if you prefer no download.

Does the distraction blocker block anything?

No. It shows a small reminder with your remaining focus time when a configured app or page comes to the front. You decide what counts as a distraction.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings → Data → Export writes a folder of Markdown files with front matter and attachments. Open it in Obsidian or any editor.

Is it free?

Dayflit is free during early access. Pricing, if any, will be announced on this page before it changes.
